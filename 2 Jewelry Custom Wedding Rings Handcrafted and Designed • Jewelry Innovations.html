    Why Do I Need an Account?

    As a manufacturer and wholesale supplier to retail jewelry stores, Jewelry Innovations only sells to authorized retailers and jewelry industry professionals.

     If you already have an account with us, to purchase from our website all you need to do is fill out the registration information, which will be sent to us for review and approval. You will receive an acknowledgement that we’ve received your request, and another when your registration is approved and your website account is activated.

     If you are a retailer but do not currently have an account with us, please fill out the registration information, and our account managers will contact you to help you become an authorized Jewelry Innovations retailer.

     Once your registration is approved, you will see your exclusive personalized wholesale pricing on all items, including trade group discounts, so you can quote accurate prices for your customers while they are in the store. Ordering from our website is fast and easy, and as always, you will receive Jewelry Innovation’s industry leading quick turnaround and customer service.
